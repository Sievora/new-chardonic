SERVICE 01

Quality Assurance & Quality Control

We provide comprehensive QA/QC management to verify that energy projects adhere to technical design specifications, safety mandates, and regulatory codes throughout construction and operational phases.

  • Site Quality Control Engineering
  • Inspection & Test Plan (ITP) Verification
  • Material Verification & Traceability
SERVICE 02

Inspection & Supervision

Deployment of certified site quality control engineers, technical supervisors, and inspectors equipped with calibrated inspection tools to ensure complete quality compliance on field operations.

  • Certified Field Supervisors
  • Welding & Coating Supervision
  • Daily Quality Documentation
SERVICE 03

Statutory Inspection & Certification

Mandatory regulatory inspection and certification of pressure equipment and lifting infrastructure to guarantee operational integrity and legal compliance.

  • Safety Relief Valves & Pressure Vessels
  • Separators, Storage Tanks & Pipeline Networks
  • Lifting Equipment Certification
SERVICE 04

Testing (NDT & Destructive)

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) and destructive testing solutions using ultrasonic testers, gamma-ray tools, pit gauges, and hardness testers to detect material flaws without compromising assets.

  • Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging
  • Gamma-Ray Radiography & Hardness Testing
  • Paint Inspection & Holiday Detection
SERVICE 05

Condition Monitoring

Proactive assessment of machinery health and asset degradation to prevent unplanned shutdowns and maximize equipment lifecycle performance.

  • Asset Integrity Monitoring
  • Vibration & Wear Diagnostics
  • Maintenance Scheduling Support
SERVICE 06

Plant & Facility Maintenance

Execution of preventive and corrective maintenance services for energy facilities, processing plants, and production units.

  • Valve & Piping Maintenance
  • Electrical & Instrumentation Overhauls
  • Mechanical Refurbishment
SERVICE 07

Oil Tool Supply

Procurement and supply of reliable oil tools, downhole equipment, inspection instruments, and specialized hardware tailored to oil and gas operations.

  • Calibrated Inspection Instruments
  • Standard Field Tool Kits
  • Industrial Petroleum Hardware
