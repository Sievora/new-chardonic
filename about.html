COMPANY OVERVIEW

Growing with the energy industry

Chardonic Oil & Gas Limited is an indigenous energy services company dedicated to advancing reliability and engineering standards across the petroleum sector.

We specialize in Quality Assurance (QA), Quality Control (QC), statutory inspection, non-destructive testing (NDT), condition monitoring, plant maintenance, and quality oil tool supply. Headquartered in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, we serve demanding energy clients with certified technical teams.
